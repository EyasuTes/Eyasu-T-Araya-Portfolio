Master’s Thesis

3D Wavelet Splatting for 3D Simulations

In progress

The thesis is about developing a compact 3D scene representation for realistic simulation and novel-view rendering. Co-authored a paper on the proposed 3D Gaussian Splatting improvements, currently under review at a top-tier computer vision conference.

Objective

Improve the visual quality and efficiency of reconstructed 3D environments used in robotics and simulation.

Approach

Uses Gabor-mixture primitives, wavelet-based optimization, and adaptive densification for detailed scene reconstruction.

Applications

Robotics simulation, autonomous navigation, digital twins, and realistic virtual environments.
